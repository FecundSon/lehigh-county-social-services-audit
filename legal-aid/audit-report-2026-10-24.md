## Headline findings — corrections strong enough to send
These are the items with own-site evidence, material to someone trying
to use the service. All other items are in the per-listing table below.

1. **North Penn Legal Services, Bethlehem office — suite corrected,
   intake line clarified.** Own site / PLAN directory give 559 Main
   Street, **Suite 200**, Bethlehem, PA 18018 — not Suite 100. Intake is
   the central line **877-953-4250**, not the local (610) 317-8757.
   Intake hours are **Mon–Thu 9:00–11:30am and 1:30–4:00pm, no Friday
   intake**; online application is available anytime. Do not publish
   aggregator office hours (Mon–Fri 9am–5pm) as own-site hours, and do
   not label the Bethlehem office as located in Lehigh County — it
   serves Lehigh and Northampton Counties. NPLS's own income table is
   from 2022 (125% of poverty); do not republish its dollar figures
   as current.
2. **Lehigh County lawyer referral is the Bar Association of Lehigh
   County, not the state bar — and it is paid, not free.** Bar
   Association of Lehigh County, Lawyer Referral Service: **1114 W.
   Walnut St., Allentown, PA 18102, (610) 433-7094**, LRS@lehighbar.org.
   Do not use the Bar's general number (610) 433-6204 for referrals, and
   do not use the secondary source's "114 West Walnut Street" (dropped
   digit). This is a paid/low-cost consultation referral to private
   attorneys, not free legal aid; the 2022 $30 half-hour consultation
   figure is unverified as current. The Pennsylvania Bar Association's
   own LRS page redirects Lehigh County to this local number; PBA's own
   number is 800-932-0311 (option 2), conflicting with the older
   1-800-692-7375 still shown on PHLP's page.
3. **Bar Modest Means Programme exists, but its own-site form is stale.**
   Reduced-fee (not free) programme via the Bar Association of Lehigh
   County, same address/phone as item 2. Its own-site application is a
   2014 upload — do not quote its income table or flat prices (e.g.
   $150 conference, $275 uncontested divorce) as 2026 eligibility/fees.
   No separate pro bono programme was found on the Bar's own site.
4. **Crime Victims Council Court Advocacy — Allentown address corrected.**
   Own site gives **2132 S. 12th Street, Suite 101, Allentown, PA 18103;
   office 610-437-6610; 24-hour hotline 610-437-6611** (distinct numbers),
   business hours Mon–Fri 8:30am–4:30pm. The guide claim 801 Hamilton
   St., Suite 300, Allentown is stale — do not use it. The programme is
   called "Court Advocacy," not a distinct "Legal Advocacy" programme.
   Its Bethlehem office (65 E. Elizabeth Ave., Suite 110) is a
   Northampton County service point; PA 211's Suite 208 / (610) 849-2446
   for it conflicts with the own site.
5. **Court Self-Help / Law Library — venue corrected.** The Lehigh County
   Government Center (17 S. 7th St.) is NOT the court/self-help venue.
   Self-Help is the Lehigh County Online Self Help Center
   (lccpa.org/Selfhelp, no legal advice, court staff cannot help fill
   out forms), and the Judge Donald E. Wieand Law Library is in the
   Historic Lehigh County Courthouse, open to the public, staff give no
   legal advice. Law Library street number, phone, and hours are
   UNVERIFIED on its own page — directory claims (501 W. Hamilton St.,
   (610) 782-3385, Mon–Fri 8:00am–4:30pm) must not be published as
   own-site-confirmed.
6. **Domestic Relations and PFA filing are court functions, not legal
   aid.** Domestic Relations Section (Title IV-D child support):
   **455 W. Hamilton Street, Room 320, Allentown, PA 18101-1614;
   610-782-3185; Mon–Fri 8:00am–4:30pm** — it does not handle custody/
   visitation and gives no legal representation; the older 10 N. 11th St.
   address is superseded. PFA filing help comes from Family Court Office
   intake staff, **Room 423, 455 West Hamilton Street, Allentown, PA
   18102, Mon–Fri 8:00am–12:15pm** (not the third-party 2:00pm window),
   phone **610-782-3390** (older brochure's 610-782-3387 treated as
   superseded). Do not attribute PFA filing to NPLS, Turning Point, or
   CVC — they support survivors around the process; they do not accept
   the petition. The courthouse ZIP discrepancy (18102 vs 18101-1614 for
   the same building) is flagged as printed, not silently fixed.
7. **Turning Point Legal Advocacy — helpline only, confidential location.**
   Legal Advocacy is an own-site programme (courthouse-based advocates
   walking survivors through the PFA process, courtroom support,
   referrals — advocacy/support, not a promise of a lawyer). Correct
   public entry only: **24/7 Helpline 610-437-3369, toll-free
   877-438-4957, TTY 610-419-4594, turningpointlv.org**. NO street
   address for it appears in this report. Directories publishing a street
   address for it are confidential-location leaks to be reported
   privately, address redacted, never republished as a correction.
8. **PA Free Legal Answers confirmed operating in Pennsylvania.**
   pa.freelegalanswers.org: virtual legal advice clinic, qualifying users
   post civil questions at no cost for pro bono attorneys. Eligibility
   on own site: household income under 250% of federal poverty level,
   liquid assets not exceeding $5,000, not incarcerated, no criminal-law
   matters, max 3 questions per year on 3 different topics. Brief advice
   only, no long-term representation, no guarantee a question will be
   answered. Do not publish a guaranteed answer timeframe (its own FAQ
   gives both 30 and 35 days in different passages).
9. **Catholic Charities immigration help is a referral, not a direct
   legal service.** Its own page heading is "Immigration Referral
   Services," phone **610-435-1541** only. No immigration office address,
   hours, fees, or eligibility appears on its own site. The directory's
   900 S. Woodward St. address is unconfirmed and conflicts with the
   402 W. Chew St. pantry site (which is a food pantry, not an immigration
   office); the diocese immigration link cited by that directory returned
   404. Its Reading office is Berks County — out of county.
10. **Lehigh University legal clinics — claim unconfirmed, contact
    corrected.** No Lehigh own-site page confirms legal clinics or an
    NPLS partnership, and Lehigh's General Counsel page says the
    university does NOT provide legal services. Correct CSO contact is
    **inserve@lehigh.edu / 610-758-6674** (not "incas@lehigh.edu").
    Do not publish this as a current clinic. CSO address is Bethlehem,
    PA 18015, county unstated on own pages — do not assume Lehigh County.
11. **Statewide services must not be presented as Lehigh offices.**
    Pennsylvania Legal Aid Network (118 Locust St., Harrisburg, Dauphin
    County, (717) 236-9486 or (800) 322-7572) does NOT provide direct
    representation — it refers to the county programme or PALawHelp.org;
    its directory hours (Mon–Fri 8:30am–4:30pm) are not on its own contact
    page. PALawHelp.org is a referral/info site with no phone found.
    Pennsylvania Health Law Project is statewide only (helpline
    1-800-274-3258, Mon & Wed 8am–8pm intake, free; offices only in
    Philadelphia and Pittsburgh; income threshold unverified). Disability
    Rights Pennsylvania is statewide toll-free intake only — (800)
    692-7443, TDD 877-375-7139, intake@disabilityrightspa.org — with a
    "Live Intake Hours are Changing!" banner whose new hours could not
    be fetched; do not publish the old Mon–Fri 9am–3pm hours as current,
    and do not publish either conflicting Harrisburg street address as
    verified. PHRC serves Lehigh from its Harrisburg Regional Office
    (555 Walnut St., Suite 808, Harrisburg, PA 17101, 717-787-9780;
    complaints 717-787-4410, phrc@pa.gov) — there is no Lehigh PHRC office,
    and Lehigh County's own separate Human Relations Commission
    (610-782-3565) must not be merged into it.
12. **Two listings that are not individual legal help at all.**
    Lehigh Valley Justice Institute is a research/policy/advocacy
    organisation (own site: Allentown, at United Way of the Greater
    Lehigh Valley, 484-544-4125, info@lvji.org) with no individual intake
    or legal-advice service — OUT OF SCOPE as a direct-service listing;
    do not publish the aggregator's Bethlehem address for it. Lehigh
    County Aging & Adult Services (17 S. 7th St., Allentown,
    610-782-3200) offers information, benefits help, care management,
    Ombudsman, and PA MEDI counselling on its own programme list — no
    legal service, advice, or representation appears; do not list it as
    a legal-aid provider without that qualification. The DA Victim/Witness
    Unit (455 W. Hamilton St., 3rd Floor, Allentown, unit phone
    610-782-3890, DA main 610-782-3100, 8:30am–4:30pm) is support/
    information/referral for people in criminal cases, not legal

## What this report does NOT claim
- It does not claim any organisation is closed or defunct. A failed page
  load, a dead link, or an unconfirmed listing is reported as UNVERIFIED,
  never as closure. In particular: Lehigh Valley Nonprofit Legal
  Corporation and the Lehigh University clinics are UNVERIFIED, not
  closed; the Catholic Charities diocese immigration link returning 404
  is a dead link, not a closure finding; DRP's new intake hours are
  unknown because a banner page failed to fetch, not because intake ended.
- It does not publish intake hours as office hours, or office hours as
  intake hours (NPLS is the clearest case: Mon–Thu intake only, no Friday
  intake).
- It does not publish stale figures as current: NPLS's 2022 income table,
  the Bar's 2022 $30 consultation fee and 2014 Modest Means income/prices,
  DRP's old 9am–3pm intake hours, and PHLP's old PBA number are all flagged
  as dated, not republished as 2026 facts.
- It does not present paid referral, court/administrative services,
  victim support, research/advocacy, or referral-only services as free
  legal aid. Items 6, 7, 11, 12, 13, 17, 21, 23, and 24's generic pointer
  are qualified in the table for exactly that reason.
- It does not give legal advice, and it does not summarise or rewrite
  any organisation's legal advice. It audits contact information only.
- It does not give a street address for Turning Point, and it does not
  give a Lehigh County location to any Bethlehem listing whose county is
  unstated on its own site.
- A live website does not by itself prove a service is currently operating
  at the listed address or hours.
