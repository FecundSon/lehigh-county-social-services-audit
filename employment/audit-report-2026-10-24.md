# Lehigh County, PA — Employment / Job Help Audit Report
Date checked: 2026-10-24
County: Lehigh County, PA. Category: employment / job help.
Method: pilot list built from public directories (PA 211, CareerLink /
Workforce Board lists, job-coaching lists); every item then checked
against the organisation's own website/page, via public web search and
page-text fetches. Own site beats directory; directory beats a
third-party aggregator. Raw checks are in checks-batch1.md,
checks-batch2.md, and checks-batch3.md (same folder).
Status: NOT SENT YET. Nothing in this report has been sent to PA 211 or
to any organisation. Keith's accept checkpoint comes before any outward

## Headline findings — corrections strong enough to send
These are the items with own-site evidence, material to someone trying
to use the service. All other items are in the per-listing table below.

1. **PA CareerLink at St. Luke's Sacred Heart — hours corrected.**
   Own site (careerlinklehighvalley.org, centres page): 325 North Fifth
   Street, Allentown, PA 18102, 610-440-4430, Mon–Fri 8am–4pm, Wed
   10am–4pm. Place listings that add 8am–4:30pm closings Tue–Thu are
   wrong — use the own-site 8am–4pm.
2. **718 Hamilton St is NOT a third CareerLink centre.**
   CareerLink's own centres page lists only two centres: 555 Union Blvd
   and St. Luke's Sacred Heart. 718 Hamilton St is the LCCC Allentown
   site where ESL and GED interest forms are taken. Directories
   listing an "Allentown Employment and Training Center" at 718
   Hamilton St (place-listing phone 610-799-1205) as a CareerLink
   centre should be corrected, and the place-listing "Possibly
   Temporarily Closed" flag must not be read as a closure finding.
3. **Workforce Board Lehigh Valley is not a second walk-in centre.**
   Its own site gives the same address and phone as CareerLink —
   555 Union Boulevard, Allentown, PA 18109, 610-437-5627 — because
   it is the board/operator behind CareerLink. Listing it as a
   separate job centre double-counts one service.
4. **Via of the Lehigh Valley — two sites, two functions, do not merge.**
   PA 211's employment-services site is 1020 S Cedar Crest Blvd,
   Allentown, PA 18103, 610-317-2326 ext 8000; Via HQ is 336 W Spruce
   St, Bethlehem, PA 18018, 610-317-8000. The Spruce St site is in
   Lehigh County (per CauseIQ's county record), so Via belongs in a
   Lehigh list — but Cedar Crest must not be presented as HQ, nor
   Spruce St as the employment intake.
5. **Bethlehem/Easton county trap, again — "Lehigh Valley" in a name
   is not Lehigh County.**
   - Hispanic Center Lehigh Valley, 520 E 4th St, Bethlehem, is in
     Northampton County (FFIEC record) — out of scope for a strict
     Lehigh-only list, despite serving the Lehigh Valley. Its 2016
     CareerLink satellite opening is also not evidence of a current
     CareerLink site.
   - AARP Foundation SCSEP, 10 E Church St, Bethlehem, is in
     Northampton County (City of Bethlehem documents) — out of scope
     as a site, even though the 55+ paid-training programme itself
     is real (toll-free 855-850-2525).
   - Private Industry Council "of Lehigh Valley", 72 N 2nd St, is in
     Easton, Northampton County — out of scope.
   - AHEDD's listed address, 3300 Trindle Rd, Camp Hill, is in
     Cumberland County — regional only, not a Lehigh listing.
6. **Goodwill, PA Inclusive, SPIN, and Merakey — no own-site local
   walk-in confirmed.**
   - Goodwill Keystone Area employment services are referral- and
     funding-based via 800-432-4483 across 22 counties; no Allentown
     employment office address is on its own page. Do not present a
     Goodwill store (1901 Lehigh St, third-party only) as an
     employment office.
   - PA Inclusive's own HQ is in Pittston (570-947-6372); its
     Marcon Blvd, Allentown, regional office is place-listing only.
   - SPIN (2158 Ave C, Bethlehem) and Merakey (3864 Adler Pl,
     Bethlehem) have no own-site Bethlehem employment office/phone
     found in these searches — both are UNVERIFIED, not closed.
7. **The Literacy Center and LCCC — address details corrected.**
   The Literacy Center HQ is 532 Hamilton St, Allentown, PA 18101
   (own site), phone 610-435-0680 ext 804; 932 Hamilton St is a
   job-training class location, not HQ, and directory hours are not
   on the centre's homepage (it defers to its Contact page).
   LCCC Allentown is 718 Hamilton St, Allentown, PA 18101 (own
   orientation page, not 18102); do not publish place-listing phone
   610-799-1940 as an Allentown direct line — the college's own
   main number is 610-799-2121 and workforce contact is
   workforce@lccc.edu.
8. **CACLV and CADA are not employment help.**
   Community Action Lehigh Valley's own site lists shelter, food,
   housing counselling, weatherization, and youth work — no distinct
   job-preparation programme. Community Action Development
   Corporation (Allentown) is neighbourhood revitalisation, not job
   help, and its place-listing phone/hours are not own-site-confirmed.

## What this report does NOT claim
- It does not claim any organisation is closed or defunct. SPIN and
  Merakey are UNVERIFIED from own sites in these searches — that is a
  gap in public web evidence, not a finding.
- It does not claim a programme exists because a directory lists it.
  Easterseals Allentown, CACLV, and CADA are all real organisations
  whose employment-help status was not own-site-confirmed; listing
  them under employment would misdirect job seekers.
- It does not publish a funder/operator as a second centre
  (Workforce Board), a class site as a headquarters (Literacy Center
  932 Hamilton St; LCCC Donley), or a regional HQ as a local office
  (PA Inclusive Pittston; AHEDD Camp Hill).
- It does not settle the county of the Arc's Ave A site or Access
  Services' Township Line Rd site from own sites — both serve the
  Lehigh Valley, both are flagged county-unresolved, neither is
  assumed to be Lehigh-only.
- It does not give employment or benefits advice. It audits contact
  information only: names, addresses, phones, hours, and intake routes
  as organisations themselves state them.
- It gives no street address for any domestic-violence safe house.
  No DV service is in this category, and none was needed.
