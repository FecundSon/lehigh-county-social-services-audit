## Rules applied to this list
- No organisation, PA 211, or anyone else was contacted.
- Own site beats directory. Directory beats a third-party aggregator.
- No claim that an organisation is closed/defunct is made from a failed
  page load; such items are UNVERIFIED.
- A funder/operator name is not a second walk-in centre; a programme
  listed under a funder's name rather than the provider's name is a
  duplicate risk, flagged, not double-counted.
- Commercial staffing/recruitment firms are NOT counted as public
  employment help (see Excluded section).
- No safe-house or confidential location is in this file. No organisation
  in this category is a domestic-violence service, so the DV rule was not
  triggered, but it was applied as a check throughout.
- This audit does not refer to Keith as a local resident.

## Excluded from this employment audit (not public employment help)
- Commercial staffing/recruitment firms with Allentown/Bethlehem offices
  (Express Employment Professionals, ProLogistix, Tricor Staffing,
  Aston Carter, Bear Staffing, Temp Poole, etc.) — these are employers
  and recruiters, not free public employment-help services. They are not
  counted, and must not be presented as PA 211-style help.
- Individual employers' hiring pages and job boards (Indeed, LinkedIn,
  etc.) — not services.
- Animal shelters and other unrelated listings — out of scope entirely.
