# Lehigh County, PA — Employment Help Listing Audit

**Checked: 2026-10-24. Status: published here first for statewide PA 211
and the organisations to respond to. Nothing in this audit has been sent
to PA 211 or any organisation as a direct message.**

## What this is
A small, dated audit of 24 employment-help listings for Lehigh County,
PA, checked against the organisations' own websites and other public
listings. It is a curation audit, not a replacement directory: it does
not claim to be a current list of open services, and it should not be
used as one without checking with the organisation or PA 211 first.

## Headline corrections (from the full audit)
1. CareerLink Lehigh Valley has two centres, not three. Full-service:
   555 Union Blvd., Allentown, 610-437-5627. Satellite: St. Luke's
   Sacred Heart, 325 N. 5th St., Allentown, 610-440-4430, Mon–Fri
   8am–4pm, Wed 10am–4pm. 718 Hamilton St. is NOT a third centre — it
   is the LCCC Allentown site for ESL/GED interest forms.
2. Workforce Board Lehigh Valley is the operator at CareerLink's
   address/phone, not a second walk-in centre.
3. Via of the Lehigh Valley: PA 211 employment site 1020 S. Cedar Crest
   Blvd., Allentown, 610-317-2326 ext. 8000, is separate from HQ 336 W.
   Spruce St., Bethlehem, 610-317-8000 — do not merge the two.
4. Goodwill employment is referral-based via 800-432-4483, with no
   Allentown office on its own page; 1901 Lehigh St. is third-party only.
5. LCCC Allentown is 718 Hamilton St., Allentown, PA 18101 (not 18102),
   workforce@lccc.edu.
6. Literacy Center HQ is 532 Hamilton St., Allentown, PA 18101;
   932 Hamilton St. is a class site, not HQ.
7. Allentown Public Library is a library with librarian appointments,
   not a job centre.
8. CACLV and CADA are not employment help (shelter/food/housing and
   neighbourhood revitalisation respectively).
9. Out of scope (Northampton County / elsewhere): Hispanic Center
   Lehigh Valley (Bethlehem), AARP Foundation SCSEP (Bethlehem),
   Private Industry Council of Lehigh Valley (Easton), AHEDD
   (Camp Hill, Cumberland County).

## Method and limits — please read before using this
- Public web search and page-text fetch only, 2026-10-24. No
  organisation, PA 211, or anyone else was contacted for this audit.
- An organisation's own website is treated as authoritative over
  third-party directories.
- **No organisation is called defunct or closed in this audit.** A
  failed or missing page is reported as unverified, never as closure.
- SPIN Employment Services and Merakey Bethlehem are UNVERIFIED from
  own sites — evidence gaps, not closure findings.
- This audit was prepared with AI assistance. Corrections, challenges,
  and updates from PA 211 or the organisations themselves are welcome —
  that is the point of publishing it here first.
