  means "not confirmed from the organisation's own site on 2026-10-24",
  including where a site was live but published no phone, address, or hours.
- It does not claim directory hours as organisation hours where the
  organisation's own site states none.
- It does not claim the Warmline numbers/hours in secondary sources are
  wrong in every digit — it claims they are unconfirmed and conflicting,
  and must be re-checked against Pinebrook's own site before use.
- It does not publish bed, placement, or waitlist availability, and it
  does not rewrite any mental-health advice.
- It does not give a street address for Turning Point, and it does not
  claim the Bethlehem offices' counties from ZIP or city names — those
  were checked by exact address.
- It does not refer to Keith as a local resident.
