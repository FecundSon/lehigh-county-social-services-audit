# Lehigh County, PA — Disability Services Listing Audit

**Checked: 2026-10-24. Status: published here first for statewide PA 211
and the organisations to respond to. Nothing in this audit has been sent
to PA 211 or any organisation as a direct message.**

## What this is
A small, dated audit of 24 disability services listings for Lehigh
County, PA, checked against the organisations' own websites and other
public listings. It is a curation audit, not a replacement directory:
it does not claim to be a current list of open services, and it should
not be used as one without checking with the organisation or PA 211
first.

## Headline corrections (from the full audit)
1. Early Intervention phone corrected: county EI office (birth–3) is
   610-782-3125, 17 S. 7th St., Allentown — not 610-782-3200, which is
   the general Human Services Information & Referral line.
2. Early-intervention age split: birth–3 = county EI; ages 3–5
   (preschool) = Carbon Lehigh IU 21, 4210 Independence Dr.,
   Schnecksville, (610) 769-4111 (EI ext. 1256). Presenting CLIU 21 as

   Allentown, PA 18103, 610-433-6018.
4. No separate BBVS Allentown office: state BBVS districts are
   Philadelphia, Wilkes-Barre, Harrisburg, Altoona, Pittsburgh, Erie
   only; Allentown has only BVRS at 45 N. 4th St. (610-821-6441 voice,
   610-821-6144 TTY). Do not create a BBVS Allentown listing by copying
   BVRS details.
5. County IDD intake clarified: 610-782-3200 (Information & Referral),
   not 610-782-3135; intake by phone, no walk-in stated.
6. LVCIL clarified: 713 N. 13th St., Allentown, PA 18102 (not 18103),
   610-770-9781; hours unverified — by appointment only.
7. Bethlehem county trap, again: The Arc (2289 Avenue A) and Via's
   registered address (336 W. Spruce St.) geocode to Lehigh County, but
   Abound Health's Bethlehem office (65 E. Elizabeth Ave., Suite 210)
   geocodes to Northampton County.
8. Good Shepherd Bethlehem sites are Northampton: a Lehigh-only list
   should carry the two Allentown outpatient sites (South Allentown
   Hyland Center, 850 S. 5th St., 610-776-3540; West Allentown, 1651 N.
   Cedar Crest Blvd., 484-788-0701) plus central 1-888-44-REHAB.
9. Statewide-only — do not invent local offices: Disability Rights
   Pennsylvania (800-692-7443), PATF (888-744-1938), TechOWL
   (800-204-7428, Lehigh access via LVCIL x123), PA Client Assistance
   Program (215-557-7112 / 888-745-2357; current mailing address
   Jenkintown, not old Philadelphia JFK Blvd. address).
10. Merakey and Step By Step are separate organisations — do not merge
    them; no Lehigh Valley Merakey location/phone was found on
    merakey.org.

## Method and limits — please read before using this
- Public web search and page-text fetch only, 2026-10-24. No
  organisation, PA 211, or anyone else was contacted for this audit.
- An organisation's own website is treated as authoritative over
  third-party directories.
- **No organisation is called defunct or closed in this audit.** A
  failed or missing page is reported as unverified, never as closure.
- Many items carry UNVERIFIED details (hours, a site address, a TTY
  number) — listed per item in the report, never published as fact.
- This audit was prepared with AI assistance. Corrections, challenges,
  and updates from PA 211 or the organisations themselves are welcome —
  that is the point of publishing it here first.
