## Headline findings — corrections strong enough to send
1. **Lehigh County Early Intervention phone — CORRECTED.**
   The county EI office (birth–3) is **610-782-3125**, 17 S 7th St,
   Allentown — not 610-782-3200. That number is the county Human
   Services Information & Referral line: a usable general route (and an
   older county book also lists it alongside statewide CONNECT
   1-800-692-7288 for new EI referrals), but it is not the EI office's
   own number.
2. **Early-intervention age split — CLARIFIED (wrong-direction risk).**
   Birth–3 is the county EI programme (item 1); **ages 3–5 (preschool)
   is Carbon Lehigh IU 21** — 4210 Independence Dr, Schnecksville,
   PA 18078, (610) 769-4111 (EI ext. 1256), serving Carbon + Lehigh.
   Directories that present CLIU 21 as birth–3 EI send families to the
   wrong programme.
3. **Center for Vision Loss is now Sights for Hope — CORRECTED.**
   Lehigh Valley office: **845 W Wyoming St, Allentown, PA 18103,
   610-433-6018** (sightsforhope.org + PA 211). Older ZIP variants
   18102 / 18102-2199 are superseded. Its Monroe County office
   (Stroudsburg, 570-992-7787) is out of Lehigh scope.
4. **No separate BBVS Allentown office — CLARIFIED.**
   The state's BBVS district list is Philadelphia, Wilkes-Barre,
   Harrisburg, Altoona, Pittsburgh, and Erie only; the OVR handbook
   lists only Allentown BVRS at 45 N 4th St. Do not create a BBVS
   Allentown listing by copying BVRS details. No BBVS referral routing
   for Lehigh was stated on pages checked.
5. **Lehigh County IDD intake — CLARIFIED.**
   Intake is **610-782-3200 (Information & Referral)** per the county's
   own enrolment page (eligibility worker calls within 2 days; supports
   coordinator within 2 weeks). **610-782-3135 is the MH/ID office
   number, not the intake number.** Intake is by phone; no walk-in
   intake is stated. Address 17 S 7th St, Allentown, confirmed.
6. **LVCIL ZIP and hours — CLARIFIED.**
   Lehigh Valley Center for Independent Living: 713 N 13th St,
   Allentown, **PA 18102** (per LVCIL's own newsletter; the county LINK
   page's 18103 appears to be a typo), 610-770-9781, info@lvcil.org.
   Hours Mon–Fri 8am–4pm are NOT own-site confirmed; LVCIL's own
   newsletter says by appointment only — hours left UNVERIFIED.
7. **Bethlehem county trap, again — CLARIFIED.**
   The Arc of Lehigh and Northampton Counties, 2289 Avenue A,
   Bethlehem, geocodes to **Lehigh** County; Via's registered address,
   336 W Spruce St, Bethlehem, also geocodes to Lehigh County. By
   contrast, **Abound Health's Bethlehem office (65 E Elizabeth Ave,
   Suite 210) geocodes to Northampton County** — a Northampton office
   serving the Lehigh Valley, including Lehigh County residents, not a
   Lehigh County office. ZIP 18018 does not settle county in either case.
8. **Good Shepherd Bethlehem sites are Northampton — CLARIFIED.**
   A Lehigh-only list should carry the two Allentown outpatient sites
   (South Allentown Hyland Center, 850 S 5th St, 610-776-3540, Mon–Fri
   7am–6:30pm, Sat 8am–12pm; West Allentown, 1651 N Cedar Crest Blvd,
   484-788-0701) plus central 1-888-44-REHAB, and label the Bethlehem
   (41 E Elizabeth Ave, 610-868-2211) and Bethlehem Township
   (3859 Nazareth Pike, 610-882-9611) sites as Northampton County.
   The 76-bed inpatient rehabilitation hospital shares the 850 S 5th St
   South Allentown campus; Hyland is the outpatient site there, not a
   separate hospital address.
9. **Statewide-only services — do not invent local offices.**
   Disability Rights Pennsylvania (statewide Live Intake
   **(800) 692-7443**; offices Harrisburg, Philadelphia, Pittsburgh
   only), Pennsylvania Assistive Technology Foundation (King of
   Prussia, (888) 744-1938 / 484-674-0506), TechOWL (Temple Institute
   on Disabilities, Philadelphia, 800-204-7428), and Pennsylvania
   Client Assistance Program (215-557-7112 / toll-free 888-745-2357,
   voice/711 relay, free, separate from OVR; current mailing address
   Jenkintown, 101 Greenwood Ave, Ste. 470, PA 19046 — an old
   Philadelphia JFK Blvd address in an old guide is outdated) have no
   Lehigh Valley office of their own. The Lehigh TechOWL access point
   is **LVCIL, Allentown (713 N 13th St, 610-770-9781 x123)**.
10. **Merakey and Step By Step are separate organisations — CLARIFIED.**
    Do not merge them. Merakey's own site publishes only national
    888-647-0020 / contact@merakey.org; no Lehigh Valley Merakey
    location/phone was found on merakey.org (a third-party Bethlehem
    listing, 3864 Adler Place, geocodes to Northampton anyway). Step
    By Step, Inc.'s own site confirms it serves the Lehigh Valley
    region, HQ Wilkes-Barre, (570) 829-3477; its local street addresses
    are not own-site confirmed.
11. **Via's second site and Terrapin House address — UNVERIFIED from
    own sites.** Via's own homepage publishes no street address or
