## Rules applied to this list
- No organisation, PA 211, or anyone else was contacted.
- An organisation's own website/page is treated as authoritative over
  third-party directories. A failed or missing page is UNVERIFIED, never
  a closure finding. No organisation is called defunct/closed.
- Contact information only is audited. No health, benefits, or legal
  advice is rewritten.
- DOMESTIC VIOLENCE: no safe-house address is published, repeated, or
  stored in these files, even if a directory lists one. No DV listing
  appears in this category.
- Bethlehem straddles Lehigh and Northampton Counties: ZIP alone does
  not settle county. County calls below rest on exact-address geocoding
  where an organisation's own site does not state county, and are
  flagged as such.
- This audit does not refer to Keith as a local resident.
