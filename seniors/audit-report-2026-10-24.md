- **PA MEDI confirmed:** Medicare/Medigap/Medicaid counseling via the
  AAA at 610-782-3200 (17 S. 7th St, Allentown); state helpline
  1-800-783-7067, Mon–Fri 8am–5pm. Old open-enrolment site numbers
  (2024/2025) deliberately not carried forward.
- **Grandparent / Older Adult Relative Caregiver Support confirmed
  with clarification:** 610-782-3200, Mon–Fri 8:30am–4:30pm; caregiver
  threshold is **55+** (not strictly over 60) and also covers adult
  relatives aged 19–59; same household, not the parent, priority for
  severe disability / greatest need.
- **In-Home Meal Service clarified:** eligibility confirmed (nutritional
  need: inability to obtain/prepare meals due to physical/cognitive
  disability, lack of money, or no one able to prepare meals; community
  resources first). No phone/hours on the programme page itself; AAA
  610-782-3200 is the general referral route, not a programme-specific
  intake number.
- **Adult Day Centers, Homemaker/Home Support, Personal Care —
  clarified:** programmes exist with own-site wording matching the
  pilot (adult day: therapeutic, protective, non-residential, caregiver
  relief, memory/physical deficits; homemaker: support to live
  independently at home; personal care: hands-on hygiene/daily-living
  help). No site, hours, eligibility, or programme-specific intake is
  published on their programme pages — general AAA 610-782-3200 only.
  Keep Homemaker/Home Support and Personal Care as two distinct
  programmes.
- **Senior-centre transport confirmed as a AAA programme note, not a
  standalone listing:** LANta Van transport to a senior centre can be
  paid by AAA — enquire via 610-782-3200. Do not conflate numbers:
  LANtaVan rider scheduling 610-253-8333 (8:00am–4:30pm, up to 14 days
  ahead, no same-day service); 65+ riders pay 15% of fare (85% PA
  Lottery) — not free for all 65+.
- **AAA office confirmed:** Lehigh County Office of Aging and Adult
  Services, Government Center, 17 S. 7th St, Allentown, PA 18101,
  agingandadult@lehighcounty.org; serves adults of all ages residing
  in Lehigh County, with benefit-application help (PACE, Property
  Tax/Rent Rebate, etc.) and listings for subsidised housing, home
  health, assisted living, personal care homes, nursing homes, and
  retirement communities.
