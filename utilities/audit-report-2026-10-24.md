# Lehigh County Utilities Assistance Audit — DRAFT FOR KEITH'S ACCEPTANCE ONLY
**County: Lehigh County, PA. Category: utilities assistance (LIHEAP,
shut-off help, fuel funds, weatherization, water/sewer assistance).**
**Date checked: 2026-10-24. Not sent to PA 211 or any organisation.**
**Method:** public web search + page-text fetch only. No organisation, PA 211,
or anyone else was contacted. An organisation's own website/page is treated
as authoritative over a directory, which beats a third-party aggregator.
A failed or missing page is reported as UNVERIFIED, never as closure.
No programme is called defunct. No benefits, eligibility, or financial
advice is rewritten here; eligibility is recorded only as the organisation
states it. Seasonal, funding, and waitlist statuses are date-stamped to
2026-10-24 and must not be presented as permanent. This report does not
refer to Keith as a local resident.

## Headline findings (the ones worth sending on, once Keith accepts)
1. **LIHEAP is closed on the check date — next season opens November 2, 2026.**
   DHS's own apply page states the 2025–26 season is now closed (cash-grant
   applications ran December 3, 2025–May 8, 2026) and the 2026–27 season is
   scheduled to open November 2, 2026. Any listing presenting LIHEAP as
   currently open, or presenting the 2025–26 season as current, is stale.
   Route confirmed: COMPASS via dhs.pa.gov/COMPASS / www.compass.dhs.pa.gov
   (older compass.state.pa.us is superseded), hotline 1-866-857-7095
   Mon–Fri, Relay 711. Crisis grants are separate from cash grants, and
   heating-system repair/replacement is a separate DCED programme
   (LIHEAP Emergency Services, typically November–March, via the County
   Assistance Office) — do not merge them into one "LIHEAP" entry.
2. **Lehigh County Assistance Office — address corrected.**
   Current DHS list: **555 Union Blvd., Suite 3, Allentown, PA 18109-3389,
   610-821-6509, toll-free 1-877-223-5956, Mon–Fri 7:30am–5pm.**
   The old PPL-list address **101 S 7th St, Allentown, PA 18101** does not
   appear for the County Assistance Office on the current DHS list and
   should be dropped.
3. **PPL phone numbers — stop listing 1-800-358-6623 as the CARES number.**
   PPL's own CARES, OnTrack, and Operation HELP routes give
   **1-800-342-5775** (and pplelectric.com/billhelp / /OnTrack).
   1-800-358-6623 is a real PPL number, but on PPL's current own site it
   is not the CARES or CAP number; it appears as the OnTrack /
   payment-arrangements line in PA PUC listings. Use 1-800-342-5775 for
   CARES, OnTrack, and Operation HELP, and list 1-800-358-6623 only as
   the PUC-listed payment-arrangements line, never as CARES.
4. **PPL weatherization — list it as WRAP, intake direct to PPL, not via
   Community Action Lehigh Valley.** The current customer-facing name is
   **WRAP** (PPL's LIURP), intake online or **1-888-232-6302 Mon–Fri
   8am–6pm**, income ≤200% of federal poverty guidelines, age 18+.
   Community Action Lehigh Valley is not on PPL's current WRAP contractor
   list (an older PUC filing did list it, so the CALV route looks
   historical). The same applies to UGI LIURP: intake is direct to UGI
   (interest form, **1-800-844-9276**, LIURPteam@ugi.com, via a UGI
   contractor), it covers **gas-heating and electric** accounts (not
   gas-only), ≤200%, and CALV is not named on UGI's own LIURP page.
5. **UGI Operation Share — applications go via the ZIP-code community
   organisation, not direct to UGI.** Funding (employee/customer
   donations + UGI corporate donation, since 1983, 501(c)(3) since 2005)
   and phone 800-276-2722 confirmed; eligibility ≤250% FPIG, residential
   gas/electric. The Salvation Army link is directory-level only (PA 211
   lists Salvation Army Lehigh Valley locally); UGI's own page names no
   Salvation Army administrator. Related correction at Salvation Army
   Lehigh Valley (144 N 8th St, Allentown): use the Social Services
   number **(610) 432-0128** alongside the PA 211 corps number
   610-432-0129, and do not publish "not eligible if already
   disconnected" as written — CAP requires an active account, but
   Operation Share funds may be used for restoration/reconnection, with
   terminated customers routed via UGI Credit Department rather than
   the community organisation.
6. **Lehigh County Authority Hardship Program — $400 is current, not $300.**
   Confirmed on both Dollar Energy's LCA page and LCA's January 2026
   document: up to **$400** per household per programme year, 200% FPL,
   at least $100 paid in the past 4 months, balance at least $200, open
   **October 1–September 30** for service off or in threat of termination,
   first-come, first-served while funding lasts. LCA Payment Plans are
   also confirmed (6- or 12-month grace period, penalties/interest
   avoided during the plan, service maintained while payments are made;
   cityservice@lehighcountyauthority.org, 610-437-7515) — note a plan
   "may be offered," not guaranteed.
7. **Good Neighbor Energy Fund — NOT a direct household application route.**
   Administered by the Lehigh Valley Community Foundation, funded by the
   PPL Foundation; 2026 grants nearly $200,000, households up to 300%
   FPL, $1,000-per-household cap (funder-ecosystem confirmed; LVCF's
   2026 release itself does not print the cap). PPL's own release:
   "Individuals interested in assistance can contact the organizations
   directly." Households must contact a recipient nonprofit — e.g. in
   Lehigh County, Lehigh Conference of Churches Housing Services
   610-439-8653 — not LVCF. Note: LVCF's 2026 recipient list is a
   Lehigh *Valley* list (both counties); New Bethany and Third Street
   Alliance on it are Northampton County, not Lehigh County routes.
8. **LIHWAP — stale, do not list as open.** The Low Income Household
   Water Assistance Program's last window was **10 July–18 August 2023**
   (after the 2022 round spent its $43.2m); no current official DHS page
   confirms a 2026 opening. Historical facts (up to $2,500 per water
   type, COMPASS/County Assistance Office, 877-395-8930 in 2023) must not
   be presented as current. Label it historical or remove it from
   current-assistance lists.
9. **Salvation Army HeatShare — UNVERIFIED for Pennsylvania/Lehigh County.**
   Salvation Army's own Allentown page confirms general utility
   assistance via Comprehensive Social Services at (610) 432-0128, but
   mentions no HeatShare, no 1-800-842-7279, and no seniors/disabled/
   medically-ill focus. Own-site HeatShare pages found are Minnesota
   and Ohio only. Do not transplant that number to Lehigh County.
10. **Two miscategorisations to fix.** (a) The PA PUC line
    **1-800-692-7380** is a regulator complaint route (Bureau of Consumer
    Services; PUC requires contacting the utility first), **not** a
    bill-payment assistance programme — list it under complaints, not
    assistance. (b) Payment arrangements / budget billing are
    all-customer options, distinct from income-based CAP/OnTrack, even
    though they share phones (PPL 1-800-358-6623 via PUC listings;
    UGI 800-276-2722, plus 800-844-9276 on UGI's assistance page).
    Hours for those lines are directory-corroborated only, not printed
    on the fetched own-site pages, so publish numbers as confirmed and
    hours as directory-reported or omit hours.

## Duplicate / same-programme-through-multiple-doors flags
- LIHEAP is one programme reached via DHS/COMPASS (item 4), the County
  Assistance Office (item 3), and CACLV's LIHEAP information pages
  (item 17). Those are three doors, not three programmes — do not
  double-count them as three sources of help.
- CAP is one programme family reached via the utility (PPL OnTrack,
  item 5; UGI CAP, item 9) and via a community administrator (Salvation
  Army Lehigh Valley for UGI, item 19). Same caution.
- Dollar Energy Fund is one fund with per-utility programme pages
  (general, item 13; LCA water/sewer, item 14). The LIHEAP/Crisis-first
  wording differs between the general page and per-utility pages.
- Good Neighbor Energy Fund (item 21) is a funder, not a household
  programme; its household route is its recipient nonprofits (e.g.
  item 20), which must not be counted twice as independent funds.

## What this report does NOT claim
- It does not claim any programme is closed or defunct. LIHEAP's closed
  2025–26 season and LIHWAP's stale status rest on positive official
  statements (DHS "season is now closed"; LIHWAP's 2023 window and
  funding exhaustion), not on failed page loads. HeatShare's
  UNVERIFIED status means only that Pennsylvania/Lehigh own-site
  confirmation was absent in this check.
- It does not claim grant availability, funding balances, or waitlist
  positions as current facts; Dollar Energy, Operation HELP, and
  Operation Share are all first-come, first-served / subject to agency
  funding, as their own sites state.
- It does not publish PPL payment-arrangement hours, UGI arrangement
  hours, or PUC hotline hours as own-site facts — those are

- It does not publish 2026–27 LIHEAP grant amounts, closing date, or a
  full income table, which DHS had not published for the new season on
