# Lehigh County, PA — Utilities Assistance Listing Audit

**Checked: 2026-10-24. Status: published here first for statewide PA 211
and the organisations to respond to. Nothing in this audit has been sent
to PA 211 or any organisation as a direct message.**

## What this is
A small, dated audit of 24 utilities-assistance listings for Lehigh
County, PA, checked against the organisations' own websites and other
public listings. It is a curation audit, not a replacement directory:
it does not claim to be a current list of open programmes, and it
should not be used as one without checking with the organisation or
PA 211 first. Seasonal, funding, and waitlist statuses are date-stamped
to 2026-10-24 and must not be presented as permanent.

## Headline corrections (from the full audit)
1. LIHEAP is closed on the check date — next season opens November 2,
   2026. 2025–26 cash grants ran Dec 3, 2025–May 8, 2026. Route: COMPASS
   via dhs.pa.gov/COMPASS, hotline 1-866-857-7095 Mon–Fri.
2. Lehigh County Assistance Office: 555 Union Blvd., Suite 3,
   Allentown, PA 18109-3389, 610-821-6509, Mon–Fri 7:30am–5pm.
   Drop the old 101 S. 7th St. address.
3. PPL phone split: PPL's own CARES / OnTrack / Operation HELP routes
   give 1-800-342-5775. 1-800-358-6623 is a real PPL number but appears
   as the OnTrack/payment-arrangements line in PUC listings, not as
   PPL's current own-site CARES number.
4. PPL WRAP (LIURP) and UGI LIURP: intake direct to the utility, not
   via Community Action Lehigh Valley (not on either current contractor
   list). UGI LIURP covers gas-heating and electric accounts, ≤200%.
5. UGI Operation Share: applications go via the ZIP-code community
   organisation, not direct to UGI. Phone 800-276-2722, ≤250% FPIG.
6. Lehigh County Authority Hardship Program: up to $400 per household
   per programme year (not $300), 200% FPL, Oct 1–Sep 30.
7. Good Neighbor Energy Fund is a funder, not a household programme:
   households must contact a recipient nonprofit directly, not the
   Lehigh Valley Community Foundation.
8. LIHWAP is stale — last window July–August 2023; do not list as open.
9. Salvation Army HeatShare is UNVERIFIED for Pennsylvania/Lehigh
   County — own-site HeatShare pages found are Minnesota and Ohio
   only. Nothing is called closed.
10. PA PUC line 1-800-692-7380 is a regulator complaint route, not
    bill-payment assistance; payment arrangements / budget billing are
    all-customer options, distinct from income-based CAP/OnTrack.

## Method and limits — please read before using this
- Public web search and page-text fetch only, 2026-10-24. No
  organisation, PA 211, or anyone else was contacted for this audit.
- An organisation's own website is treated as authoritative over
  third-party directories.
- **No programme is called defunct or closed in this audit.** A failed
  or missing page is reported as unverified, never as closure.
- No benefits, eligibility, or financial advice is rewritten here;
  eligibility is recorded only as the organisation states it.
- This audit was prepared with AI assistance. Corrections, challenges,
  and updates from PA 211 or the organisations themselves are welcome —
  that is the point of publishing it here first.
