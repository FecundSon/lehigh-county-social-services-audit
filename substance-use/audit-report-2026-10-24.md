- No organisation is called closed or defunct. Bet El, Phoenix Training
  Institute, Council on Alcohol and Drug Abuse, White Deer Run of
  Allentown, and REACH LV are UNVERIFIED from own sites, not closures;
  third-party "closed"/"permanently closed" flags were not adopted.
- No bed or placement availability is reported. Intake phone/process
  only; availability changes constantly.
- No treatment or medical advice is given or rewritten. Services are
  described only as the organisations themselves describe them
  (outpatient, residential, recovery centre, etc.).
- No schedule from an office/church page is presented as a service
  schedule, and no directory hours are published where they conflict
  with, or are absent from, the organisation's own site.
- No domestic-violence safe-house address appears in this report or
  any file in this folder; none was encountered in this category.
- This report does not refer to Keith as a local resident of
  Lehigh County.
- Bethlehem postal names do not settle county: Habit OpCo Bethlehem and
  Hogar Crea Bethlehem (Women) are Northampton, MARS (Fountain Hill) is
  Lehigh, and White Deer Run of Lehigh's county is unresolved — exact
  address, not town name, decides.
