## Headline findings — corrections strong enough to send
These are the items with own-site evidence, material to someone trying
to use the service. All other items are in the per-listing table below.

1. **CACLV ERAP page is stale — remove ERAP from live help.**
   Community Action Lehigh Valley's rental-assistance page still says
   COVID-era ERAP funds are available, with Lehigh contact Catholic
   Charities, 900 S. Woodward St, ERAP@allentowndiocese.org. PA DHS
   states ERAP1 ended 2022-09-30 and ERAP2 ended 2025-09-30, so the
   page's present-tense "funds are still available" claim must not be
   repeated as current help. (Batch 1 evidence: PA DHS ERAP page,
   pa.gov/agencies/dhs/resources/erap; CACLV page,
   communityactionlv.org/rental-assistance.)
2. **Lehigh Conference of Churches — current address is
   457 W. Allen St, Allentown, PA 18102, Housing Services
   610-439-8653.** Retire 534 W. Chew St and 544 Hamilton St as current
   addresses; both come from an old county handout listing former
   offices. The 457 W. Allen St address is the organisation's own
   event listing (Dubbs Memorial Community Center) and two recent
   guides agree.
3. **Allentown Housing Authority — own-site address form is
   1339 W. Allen Street, Allentown, PA 18102, 610-439-8678.**
   The pilot's "1339 Allen St" is the homepage's short form. The
   pilot's tenant-selection phone (610-439-0481) was NOT confirmed on
   own-site pages and must not be presented as own-site-confirmed.
   A secondary-only report that the Section 8 list is closed (last
   open Jan–Feb 2018) is dated 2026-10-24 and is NOT an own-site
   statement — do not send it as fact without an AHA check.
4. **Hispanic American Organization (HAO) — 462 W. Walnut St,
   Allentown, PA 18102, 610-435-5334, is current; 136 S. 4th St is
   superseded.** HAO's own homepage footer gives 462 W. Walnut St;
   housing case management is 8am–1pm by appointment only, bilingual
   English/Spanish. The 136 S. 4th St address appears only in an old
   secondary directory.
5. **Family Promise of Lehigh Valley Prevention Program —
   1346 Hamilton St (1346 W. Hamilton St), Allentown, PA 18102,
   610-351-1368, not 1426 Hamilton St.** 1426 appears only in a place
   listing. Prevention is three months of case management plus
   financial assistance for families just starting to fall behind on
   rent or utility bills. (Cross-listed with the shelters audit, where
   Family Promise is a family shelter; this item covers Prevention.)
6. **Catholic Charities housing help — list by phone, not by address.**
   Its own services page confirms emergency housing assistance, rental
   support, counselling, budgeting, and case management, via
   610-435-1541 only. Neither 900 S. Woodward St (the historical ERAP
   contact) nor 402 W. Chew St (the Seton Hall pantry site) is
   presented on its own services page as the housing-assistance
   office. Its SSVF programme for veterans/families (rental and
   utility assistance, housing expenses, case management) is a
   SEPARATE programme on the same phone — do not merge the two.
7. **Lehigh County HAP is ONE county fund, not three sources of help.**
   County material contracts HAP out to Catholic Charities, HAO, and
   Lehigh Conference of Churches, with no in-house funding. Items 4,
   6, and 7 above are its intake routes. No current dedicated HAP
   intake page was found; current eligibility wording rests on an
   older county presentation and was not re-verified for 2026-10-24.
8. **Rooster Relief Fund — contact corrected.** One-time help up to
   $1,500 for a rental or mortgage payment, via an authorised client
   representative only (no self-application; PA 211 screening first).
   Contacts on the April 2026 LVRHAB one-pager: Seth Campbell
   (seth.campbell@valleyhealthpartners.org) and Danielle Mineo
   (Danielle.Mineo@allentownpa.gov) — replacing the pilot list's
   bstephens@lehighchurches.org. No phone was found; phone is
   UNVERIFIED. "Available!" is dated 2026-10-24 only, not a fund
   guarantee.
9. **USDA Rural Housing — ZIP corrected, and a closer route exists.**
   Pennsylvania State Office: 359 East Park Drive, Suite 4,
   Harrisburg, PA 17110-2747 (not 17111), 717-237-2299. The closer
   Lehigh-serving route is the Northampton Area Office, 3910 Adler
   Pl., Bethlehem, PA 18017, (484) 635-3791, serving Lehigh among 10
   counties. Rural-eligibility boundaries apply; this is a regional
   route only, not a blanket Lehigh offer.
10. **Lehigh County Veterans Affairs is a referral/benefits office,
    not a direct HUD-VASH application point.** County office:
    17 S. 7th St, Allentown, PA 18101-2401, 610-782-3295, appointment
    only, no walk-ins; temporary financial-assistance grants and
    VA-benefits help. No HUD-VASH application or "fast-track" Section
    8 claim was found on county pages — HUD-VASH starts via VA
    (Allentown Outpatient Clinic homeless outreach, 610-776-4329
    ext. 2364; National Call Center 1-877-424-3838). Do not repeat the
    secondary guide's "fast-track" claim as fact.
