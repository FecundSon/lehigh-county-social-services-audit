Date started: 2026-10-24
Scope: housing and rental assistance only, serving Lehigh County. This is the
candidate list from public directories; findings are in
audit-report-2026-10-24.md (same folder). Nothing has been sent to PA 211
or any organisation. Keith's accept checkpoint comes before any outward
