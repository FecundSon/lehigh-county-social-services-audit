# Lehigh County, PA — Transportation Listing Audit

**Checked: 2026-10-24. Status: published here first for statewide PA 211
and the organisations to respond to. Nothing in this audit has been sent
to PA 211 or any organisation as a direct message.**

## What this is
A small, dated audit of 27 transportation listings for Lehigh County,
PA, checked against the organisations' own websites and other public
listings. It is a curation audit, not a replacement directory: it does
not claim to be a current list of open services, and it should not be
used as one without checking with the organisation or PA 211 first.
Fares and schedules are date-stamped to 2026-10-24 and must not be
presented as permanent.

## Headline corrections (from the full audit)
1. LANTA Day Pass is $5.00, not $4.00, in June 2026 timetables
   (Single $2.50). PA 211 and LANTA's own About Us page still say $4 —
   re-check LANTA's fares page at publication time. Seniors ride free
   with a valid PennDOT Senior Citizen ID Card (not "PA Transit ID").
   Customer service foregrounds 888-253-8333, not 610-776-7433.
2. Allentown Transportation Center is one centre, not two addresses:
   603 Linden St. (Trans-Bridge Platform C boarding) and 112 N. 6th St.
   (building/ticket-office address) are the same ATC complex at 6th &
   Linden. 325 Hamilton St. has no own-site/carrier evidence.
3. Greyhound downtown Allentown stop is not verified at 325 Hamilton
   St. Greyhound's own site still serves Allentown: a downtown
   "Allentown Bus Stop" (address map-only, unverified) plus the LVIA
   stop, 3311 Airport Road, (800) 231-2222. Drop 610-434-6188 and
   325 Hamilton St. as current.
4. LANtaFlex in Lehigh County is route 501 (Macungie) only (Mon–Sat
   6am–6pm, Macungie/Alburtis Flex Zone). PA 211's "7 LANtaFlex routes"
   is stale; 502/506 are Northampton County. Reservation phone
   conflicts on LANTA's own pages — verify a single number before
   publishing.
5. ITNLehighValley fare is stale in directories: replace "$4 pickup +
   $1.50/mile" with the Nov 1, 2024 fee schedule ($5 pickup / $6
   same-day; $2/mile pre-scheduled / $4 same-day; $10 minimum).
   Phone 610-419-1645 confirmed. Age threshold conflicts (65+ vs 60+)
   — resolve before publishing one.
6. Prime Medical Mobility phone conflict — verify before publishing:
   own site (484)-725-8395 vs directory 484-725-7395 (one digit).
7. 610-432-3200 relabelled: LANtaVan General Information / Applications
   (Mon–Fri 8am–4:30pm), not fixed-route customer service.
8. MATP is a state programme delivered in Lehigh County by
   LANTA/LANtaVan, not a separate Lehigh company. Scheduling
   610-253-8333, up to 14 days ahead, by 4:30pm day before; NO same-day
   service.
9. Carbon Transit touches Lehigh County only at Whitehall (mall area)
   — do not list it as a Lehigh County transit option.
10. Easton Coach / Easton Intermodal is OUT OF SCOPE (Northampton
    County; cross-reference only).

## Method and limits — please read before using this
- Public web search and page-text fetch only, 2026-10-24. No
  organisation, PA 211, or anyone else was contacted for this audit.
- An organisation's own website is treated as authoritative over
  third-party directories.
- **No service is called defunct or closed in this audit.** A failed
  or missing page is reported as unverified, never as closure.
- Fares and schedules change; re-check LANTA's fares page, LANtaFlex
  reservation phone, ITN age threshold, and Prime Medical Mobility
  phone before publishing those items.
- This audit was prepared with AI assistance. Corrections, challenges,
  and updates from PA 211 or the organisations themselves are welcome —
  that is the point of publishing it here first.
