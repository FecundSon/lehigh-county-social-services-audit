# Lehigh County, PA — Transportation Audit Report
Date checked: 2026-10-24
County: Lehigh County, PA. Category: transportation (public transit,
paratransit, senior/disability rides, medical transport, volunteer rides).
Method: pilot list built from public directories (PA 211 / search.pa211.org,
LANTA, Lehigh County, and other public listings); every item then checked
against the organisation's own website/page, via public web search and
page-text fetches. Own site beats directory; directory beats a third-party
aggregator. Raw checks are in checks-batch1.md (items 1–8),
checks-batch2.md (items 9–17), and checks-batch3.md (items 18–27)
(same folder).
Status: NOT SENT YET. Nothing in this report has been sent to PA 211 or
to any organisation. Keith's accept checkpoint comes before any outward
report. This audit does not refer to Keith as a local resident.

## Headline findings — corrections strong enough to send
These are the items with own-site evidence, material to someone trying
to use the service. All other items are in the per-listing table below.
Fares and schedules are dated 2026-10-24 and must not be republished as
permanent facts.

1. **LANTA Day Pass is $5.00, not $4.00, in June 2026 timetables.**
   PA 211 and LANTA's own About Us page still say $4, but LANTA route
   timetables effective 6/15/2026 show Single $2.50, Day Pass $5.00.
   Re-check LANTA's fares page at publication time, since the
   fare-change page describes the $2.50/$5.00 structure as "proposed"
   while dated timetables already carry it. Seniors ride free with a
   valid PennDOT Senior Citizen ID Card (not "PA Transit ID" wording).
   Customer service foregrounds 888-253-8333, not 610-776-7433.
   Route count: own About Us says 23 fixed + 10 special routes, vs
   PA 211's "25 local" and "38 routes" in different passages — do not
   publish "25" alone. (Batch 1, item 1.)
2. **Allentown Transportation Center is one centre, not two addresses.**
   603 Linden St (Trans-Bridge Platform C boarding) and 112 N 6th St
   (building/ticket-office address) are the same ATC complex at 6th &
   Linden. Publish as: ATC, Platform C, 603 Linden St for Trans-Bridge
   boarding; 112 N 6th St is the same centre's building address.
   325 Hamilton St has no own-site/carrier evidence — do not publish
   it as a current stop. (Batch 1, item 7; Batch 3, item 19.)
3. **Greyhound downtown Allentown stop is not verified at 325 Hamilton St.**
   Greyhound's own site still serves Allentown with 2 stops: a downtown
   "Allentown Bus Stop" (street address given only on a map, not in
   Greyhound's own text) and Lehigh Valley International Airport,
   3311 Airport Road, Allentown, PA 18109 ("wait at the LVIA Transit
   Center near the Rental Car lot", (800) 231-2222). Drop
   610-434-6188 and 325 Hamilton St as current until a ticket/map
   confirms the downtown address. Place listings marking 325 Hamilton
   St "Permanently Closed" are not treated as proof of closure.
   (Batch 3, item 19.)
4. **LANtaFlex in Lehigh County is route 501 (Macungie) only.**
   LANTA's own site lists only 3 Flex routes: 501 Macungie Flex
   (Lehigh County, Mon–Sat 6am–6pm, Macungie/Alburtis Flex Zone),
   502 Slate Belt Flex and 506 Bethlehem-Gracedale Flex
   (Northampton County). PA 211's "7 LANtaFlex routes" is stale.
   Booking: reservation-based curb-to-curb shared ride, at least
   2 hours prior (previous Friday for Sat/Sun/Mon-morning), max
   14 days ahead, agents Mon–Fri 8am–4pm. Reservation phone is
   inconsistent on LANTA's own pages (888-253-8333 vs 888-287-8818)
   — verify a single number before publishing. (Batch 1, item 3.)
5. **ITNLehighValley fare in directories is stale, and its own site
   disagrees with itself on age.** Replace "$4 pickup + $1.50/mile"
   with the Nov 1, 2024 fee schedule figures (dated 2026-10-24):
   pickup $5.00 (7am–7pm), $6.00 same-day; $2.00/mile pre-scheduled
   (by 2pm day before), $4.00/mile same-day; $10 minimum one-way;
   $100 individual / $130 family annual membership; no wheelchair
   vans (collapsible transport wheelchair only if rider can
   self-transfer). Own Ride page says 65+; fee schedule says 60+ —
   resolve before publishing an age threshold. Phone 610-419-1645
   confirmed. (Batch 2, item 9.)
6. **Prime Medical Mobility phone conflict — verify before publishing.**
   Own site footer gives (484)-725-8395; directory gives
   484-725-7395 (8395 vs 7395, one digit). Do not publish both.
   Street address 645 N Ninth St is place listing only, not
   own-site confirmed. (Batch 2, item 14.)
7. **LANtaVan General Information number relabelled.**
   610-432-3200 (in the PA 211 record beside 610-776-7433) is a
   LANTA number, but LANtaVan General Information / Applications,
   Mon–Fri 8am–4:30pm — distinct from 610-253-8333 (LANtaVan
   reservations) and 888-253-8333 (customer service). Keep it,
   relabelled. (Batch 1, item 8.)
8. **MATP is a state programme delivered in Lehigh County by
   LANTA/LANtaVan — not a separate Lehigh company.** MA ACCESS card
   holders use LANtaVan for MA-reimbursable appointments; scheduling
   610-253-8333, 8am–4:30pm, up to 14 days ahead, by 4:30pm day
   before; NO same-day service. (Batch 3, item 26; Batch 1, item 2.)
9. **Carbon Transit touches Lehigh County only at Whitehall.**
   LANTA manages Carbon County's system; CT runs within Carbon
   County and "to several points outside of the county including
   the regional Mall area in Whitehall, Lehigh County." Do not list
   it as a Lehigh County transit option; at most note the Whitehall
   cross-boundary touch. (Batch 1, item 4.)
10. **Easton Coach / Easton Intermodal is OUT OF SCOPE for Lehigh.**
    Easton Coach is a charter/school/paratransit carrier, HQ
    1200 Conroy Pl, Easton, PA, 610-253-4055, with no scheduled
    public Lehigh County boarding service evidenced. Easton
    Intermodal (123 S 3rd St, Ste. 200, Easton) is a separate
    Northampton facility whose Trans-Bridge/Greyhound/LANTA routes
    connect to Lehigh stops — keep at most a cross-reference, not
    a Lehigh item. (Batch 3, item 25.)

## What this report does NOT claim
- No organisation or service in this report is called closed or defunct.
  Items marked UNVERIFIED (ShareCare phone/hours; Lehigh Valley Medical
  Transportation; Genesis Medical Transport; FOCUS EMS/Transport; Quick
  Service Taxi; Lehigh Valley Transportation Services) are gaps in
  public own-site evidence, not findings, and must not be published as
  fact or as closures. Place-listing "Permanently Closed" flags for
  Greyhound's old terminal and for Lehigh Valley Transportation
  Services were explicitly not treated as proof of closure.
- Fares and schedules are dated 2026-10-24. LANTA's $5.00 Day Pass /
  $2.50 single, Trans-Bridge's $65.00 cash fare, ITN's Nov 2024 fee
  schedule, and all route hours must be re-checked before republication;
  routes, fares, and contract operators change.
- No bed, vehicle, or ride availability is reported as a static fact.
- No eligibility or benefits advice is rewritten here; MATP, LANtaVan,
  and senior-ride eligibility are reported only as the organisations
  themselves state them.
- No domestic-violence safe-house address appears in this report.
  No confidential location was encountered in this category.
- A live website does not by itself prove a service is currently
  operating (see items 23–24, and the GO Program's undated launch
  article, item 10).
