# Lehigh County, PA — Child Care / Family Support Audit Pilot
Date started: 2026-10-24 — AUDIT IN PROGRESS, awaiting Keith's accept checkpoint.
Scope: child care centres, Pre-K / Head Start, early intervention, and
family support serving Lehigh County. This file is the candidate list from
public directories; findings are in audit-report-2026-10-24.md (same folder)
and the raw checks in checks-batch1–3.md. Nothing has been sent to PA 211
or any organisation.

## Rules applied to this list
- No organisation, PA 211, or anyone else was contacted.
- Own site beats directory. Directory beats a third-party aggregator.
- A failed or missing page is UNVERIFIED, never a closure finding.
- Audit contact information only: name, phone, address IF public, ages
  served, subsidy/assistance route IF stated on own site, hours,
  waitlist status IF stated on own site. Do NOT rewrite child care,
  health, or benefits advice. Do NOT record any information about
  individual children.
- Large multi-site providers (LVCC, CSC) are counted ONCE as an
  organisation, with example sites noted separately — do not count
  every site as a separate listing, and do not double-count a provider
  under two programme names unless the programmes are genuinely
  separate listings.
- DOMESTIC VIOLENCE: no safe-house address is published, repeated, or
  stored in these files, even if a directory lists one. DV listings
  record helpline numbers and public services only.
- No claim that Keith is a local resident of Lehigh County is made.

## Anchor sources
- search.pa211.org (PA 211 listings, e.g. Community Services for Children
  Head Start / Early Head Start record)
- Public directories (countyoffice.org / daycare.com Allentown and

  (unless listed as a PA 211 child care listing) — not counted here.

  out of scope.
- Family medical practices (e.g. Lehigh Valley Family Practice) —
  medical, not child care / family support as listed here.
